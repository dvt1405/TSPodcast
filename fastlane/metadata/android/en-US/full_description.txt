TSPodcast lets you listen to Vietnamese and international podcasts, live radio and open music feeds in one app. Pick the topics you like and the app opens on shows that match, ready to play, with no account needed.

<b>Discover podcasts every day</b>
• Trending podcasts, Vietnamese shows first, updated continuously from the open Podcast Index directory
• Pick your favourite topics from more than 100 (news, business, technology, education, history, health, comedy, sports, stories…) and "You may like" suggests shows to match
• Search podcasts by show name or keyword
• Live podcasts: listen to episodes streaming right now

<b>Live radio</b>
• Listen live to VOV and VOH radio channels
• Tap to play, radio right on your phone

<b>Open music library</b>
• Music feeds published by the artists themselves (Podcasting 2.0)
• A "Music" filter right in search

<b>Listen your way</b>
• Background playback: keep listening with the screen off or in other apps
• Controls in the notification, on the lock screen and on Bluetooth headphones
• Mini player while you keep browsing
• Episode list to switch episodes quickly
• Save favourite episodes in one place
• No full-screen ads while you are listening

<b>Made for Vietnamese listeners</b>
• Vietnamese interface
• Spam and gambling shows filtered out by a regularly updated block list

TSPodcast is free and ad-supported. TSPodcast is an independent app, not affiliated with any radio station or podcast producer. Podcast and music content belongs to its creators; directory data is provided by Podcast Index.
