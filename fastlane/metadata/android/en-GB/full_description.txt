TSPodcast helps you discover podcasts worth your time and listen the way you like. Trending shows, suggestions based on the topics you pick, and a player with speed control, sleep timer and resume, all without an account.

<b>Discover podcasts</b>
• Trending podcasts in the language you choose, plus English, updated continuously from the open Podcast Index directory
• Choose from more than 100 topics (news, business, technology, education, history, health, comedy, sports, fiction…) to personalise your suggestions
• Search by show name or keyword, with shows in your language first
• Search inside a show to find the right episode
• Live podcasts: listen to episodes streaming right now

<b>Listen your way</b>
• Playback speed from 0.5× to 3.0×, remembered for each show
• Sleep timer: 5 to 60 minutes or at the end of the episode
• Skip back 15 seconds and forward 30 seconds
• Pick up every episode where you left off
• Background playback with controls in the notification, on the lock screen and on Bluetooth headphones
• Mini player while you keep browsing
• App shortcuts: long-press the app icon to continue listening, pause or start a 30-minute sleep timer

<b>Music and radio</b>
• Open music library: music feeds published by independent artists (Podcasting 2.0), with a Music filter in search
• Vietnamese radio: listen live to radio channels from Vietnam

<b>Your library, your choice</b>
• Save favourite episodes in one place
• Hide shows you don't want to see, and bring them back any time in Settings
• Report a show or episode that breaks the rules
• Share links to shows and episodes
• Spam and gambling shows filtered out by a regularly updated block list

<b>Your language, your privacy</b>
• App in 15 languages, and podcast lists follow the language you choose
• Liquid Glass look with translucent bars, mini player and buttons, which you can turn off in Settings
• Analytics and crash reports are sent only with your consent, and you can change your choice any time

TSPodcast is an independent app, not affiliated with any podcast producer or radio station. Podcast and music content belongs to its creators; directory data is provided by Podcast Index.

Feedback, bug reports or content removal requests: email the support address on this page. Every message is read.

TSPodcast is free, no account needed.
