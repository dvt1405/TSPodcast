Delete your TSPodcast data

Updated 1 November 2026.

Đọc bằng tiếng Việt

TSPodcast has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is how to delete data related to the app.

1. Delete data on your phone

Favourites, listening history, playback positions, playback speeds, hidden shows, chosen topics and consent choices are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → TSPodcast → Storage → Clear data; or
  2. Uninstall TSPodcast.

Either way removes all on-device data. The next launch uses a new installation ID.

2. Stop sending analytics and crash data

Open the app → Settings → turn off usage analytics and crash reporting. The app stops sending new data to Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

To change ad choices, use "Tùy chọn quyền riêng tư và quảng cáo" (Privacy and ad options) in the app, or reset/delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Request deletion of data already sent

Email admin@tuandv.me with the subject "TSPodcast data deletion request" and include:

  • your phone model and Android version;
  • your TSPodcast app version;
  • roughly when you used the app.

We delete the analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash (Firebase Crashlytics) data tied to installation IDs we can identify, and any emails you sent us if you ask, and reply within the time limits set by law. Data we cannot identify is deleted automatically when its retention period ends: 14 months for Analytics, 90 days for Crashlytics.

4. Data held by others

Advertising data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Podcast Index, podcast publishers and radio stations keep their own access logs; please contact them directly.

Full details: Privacy Policy, Deleting your data.
