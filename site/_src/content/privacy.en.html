1. Who we are and scope

TSPodcast is an app for listening to podcasts, online radio and music, built by an independent developer based in Vietnam ("we"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: admin@tuandv.me.

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the TSPodcast app, why, who receives it and what rights you have, in line with Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 (in force from 1 January 2026), its implementing regulations, and the Google Play User Data policy.

TSPodcast is an independent app and is not affiliated with any radio station or podcast producer. Podcast and music content belongs to its creators; directory data is provided by Podcast Index.

2. Data stored on your device

The app has no accounts and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or GPS location. The following data is kept only in the app's storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • favourite episodes and shows;
  • listening history and the playback position of each episode (to resume);
  • the playback speed chosen for each show and the sleep timer;
  • shows you have hidden, topics you have picked, and the setting to hide explicit content;
  • your consent choices for analytics, crash reporting and ads.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings (Apps → TSPodcast → Storage → Clear data). If Android backup is turned on, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under Google's policies.

3.2 Advertising: Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The Android app shows Google ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, this SDK and Google UMP may process:

  • your Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, OS, language, carrier) and interactions with ads;
  • your consent choices.

Where required, the app shows Google's UMP consent form before loading ads. You can change your choices through "Tùy chọn quyền riêng tư và quảng cáo" (Privacy and ad options) in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path varies by device).

Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3.3 Third-party content servers

  • Podcast Index (podcastindex.org): when you open lists, browse topics or search, the app sends requests (including your search terms) to Podcast Index to fetch directory data. Podcast Index receives your IP address with each request.
  • Podcast and music publishers: audio, artwork and feeds are loaded directly from servers chosen by the publisher. Those servers see your IP address and player information (user agent).
  • Radio stations: radio streams come from the stations' servers, which also see your IP address.

These parties act independently of us and their own privacy policies apply.

3.4 Email you send us

If you send feedback, a bug report or a content report by email, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to and handle your request.

5. Retention

  • On-device data: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level event data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Firebase Installation ID and push token: until the app is uninstalled or the identifier expires under Google's policies.
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention periods, see How Google retains data.
  • Email: as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Data is processed only by the parties listed in section 3: Google LLC (Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, UMP), acting as processor or independent controller under Google's terms, and the content servers you reach when you listen. We disclose data to public authorities only when the law requires it.

7. International transfers

Servers run by Google (Firebase, AdMob), Podcast Index and many podcast publishers are located outside Vietnam, for example in the United States, so the data described in section 3 is transferred abroad. Google applies safeguards such as encryption in transit and the data protection commitments in its data processing terms. We carry out the procedures for cross-border transfer of personal data required by Vietnamese law.

8. Security

Data sent to Firebase and the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On-device data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Access to the Firebase console is limited to the developer. Some podcast publishers and radio stations still stream over unencrypted HTTP; in that case the connection to their server is not encrypted.

9. Children

TSPodcast is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app filters content marked as explicit by default. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

  • be informed about the processing of your personal data (this policy);
  • give or refuse consent, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • access, obtain and correct your personal data;
  • request deletion, restrict processing or object to processing;
  • complain, report, take legal action and claim compensation as provided by law.

How to exercise them:

  • Withdraw consent to analytics and crash reporting: turn them off in the app's Settings.
  • Change ad choices: use "Tùy chọn quyền riêng tư và quảng cáo" in the app, or reset/delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Turn off notifications: Android Settings → Apps → TSPodcast → Notifications.
  • Anything else: email admin@tuandv.me. We reply within the time limits set by law. Because the app has no accounts, we may need some extra information (see section 11) to find the right data.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the personal data protection authority under Vietnam's Ministry of Public Security.

11. Deleting your data

  • There are no accounts, so there is no account to delete.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes all on-device data (section 2) and gives the next installation new identifiers.
  • Turn off analytics and crash reporting in Settings to stop new data from being sent to Firebase.
  • To request deletion of analytics and crash data already sent, email admin@tuandv.me with the subject "TSPodcast data deletion request" and include your device model, app version and roughly when you used the app. We will delete the data tied to installation IDs we can identify. Data we cannot identify is deleted automatically when the periods in section 5 end.

12. iOS app

When released, the TSPodcast iOS app contains no advertising SDK. Analytics and crash reporting on iOS are switched on only with your consent and can be turned off in the app's Settings → Privacy. The rest of this policy applies as it does on Android.

13. This website

podcast-25c93.web.app does not use cookies and does not run analytics or advertising tools. The hosting provider (Google Firebase Hosting) may keep technical access logs, such as IP address and browser, to operate and protect the service.

14. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change significantly affects your rights, we will tell you in the app or on the Google Play listing and ask for consent again where needed.
